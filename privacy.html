Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information AI Agents HQ collects through this website and our demo phone line, and what we do with it.

Who we are

AI Agents HQ builds AI agents and automations for small businesses. You can reach us at hello@aiagentshq.xyz.

What we collect

  • Contact form. When you send us a note, we receive your name, email, and anything else you choose to add, such as your business name, phone number and message.
  • Demo phone line. Calls to (380) 268-1020 are answered by an AI receptionist, and she says so. Calls are recorded and transcribed so we can review how the call was handled. If you book a call with us during the conversation, we keep the name, company and email you give.
  • Booked calls. When you book a call through our scheduling link, we receive the name and email you enter, and a calendar invite is sent to you.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can reply.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't run advertising or analytics trackers on this website.
  • We don't add you to a mailing list because you called or wrote to us.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, to hold the call you booked, and to improve how our AI receptionist handles calls. That's it.

Services that help us run things

We rely on a few outside services, and your information passes through them when you use the related feature:

  • Retell AI and Twilio, for the demo phone line
  • Google, for email, calendar invites and the fonts on this site
  • Calendly, for booking calls
  • Our own server, which receives the contact form and forwards it to our inbox

If you become a client

When we run an AI receptionist for your business, calls from your customers are handled on your behalf. We use that information only to provide the service to you, such as booking the appointment and sending you the call summary.

Keeping and deleting

We keep enquiries and call records for as long as they are useful for replying to you or providing a service. If you want us to delete what we hold about you, email hello@aiagentshq.xyz and we will.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
