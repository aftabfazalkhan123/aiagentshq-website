FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will my customers know it's an AI?

Yes. Ava introduces herself as an AI receptionist. Callers care that someone picks up, answers their question, and gets them booked. That's what she does.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You can forward your existing business line to Ava, all the time or only when you don't pick up. You can also give her a new number if you prefer.

What if she can't answer something?

She takes the caller's name, number and question, tells them you'll follow up, and you get the details in your call summary. She never makes up prices or promises.

Which calendar does she book into?

Google Calendar out of the box. Other calendars and CRMs can usually be connected during setup.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within a week. We gather your services, hours, service area and common questions on a short call, then build, test and hand it over.

What happens if I go over 300 minutes?

Extra minutes are $0.35 each, billed with your monthly plan. A typical call runs two to three minutes, so 300 minutes covers around 100 to 150 calls a month.
