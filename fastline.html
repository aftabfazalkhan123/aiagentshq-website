FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this work with my email and price list?

Yes. Fastline connects to Gmail or Outlook and reads from the price sheet you already keep. Google Sheets works out of the box. If your prices live in a CRM or ERP with an API, it can usually be connected during setup.

Can I check quotes before they go out?

Yes. By default Fastline replies on its own, but an approval step can be added so a person on your team signs off on every quote before it reaches the buyer.

What if my pricing logic is complicated?

Bulk discounts, tiered pricing, margins and shipping rules can be built into the logic during setup, based on how you actually price today. We scope this on the call before anything is built.

How long does setup take?

Most builds are ready to test within a week, depending on how your pricing and product data is structured.

Is my data secure?

Your data stays in your own accounts and systems. Fastline connects to them, it does not copy your pricing or customer data anywhere else.

What happens after setup is done?

You own the workflow. The optional $300 monthly plan covers hosting, monitoring, and adjustments as your pricing or product list changes.
