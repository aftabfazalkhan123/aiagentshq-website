FAQ

Partner questions

Who deals with the client?

On a referral, we do. On white-label, you do, and we stay in the background.

What does it cost?

Referred clients pay the price on our AI Receptionist page. White-label pricing is agreed with you on a call, and you set what your client pays.

Will callers know it's an AI?

Yes. The receptionist introduces itself as an AI. Callers care that someone picks up, answers their question, and gets them booked.

What does the client need?

Their existing business number and a Google Calendar. Other calendars and CRMs can usually be connected during setup.
